How to get the best accuracy

Put the phone about 2–3 metres away so your whole body is visible. Use good lighting and a contrasting background. Keep the phone upright and roughly at waist/chest height. The counter uses your body trajectory, so the rope itself does not have to be visible.

Tip: start the camera, stand still for a second, then begin skipping. The algorithm looks for a complete upward → downward jump cycle and rejects very short movements.

Privacy

Pose detection and counting run in your browser. This prototype does not upload your camera recording to a server.
